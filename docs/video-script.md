# The demo video, 95 seconds

Six shots, screen capture only, English narration, captions on. Numbers in brackets are placeholders until the final run; every one of them comes from the app or from the route endpoint.

## Format

1920 by 1080, 30 fps, screen recording of the running app, no slides. Voice from a synthesised track, attached as audio. Captions burned in, because people watch muted in a noisy hall. The file is uploaded to YouTube as unlisted and the link goes in the submission; there are no video file uploads.

## Recording order

1. Record the whole run once at real speed as a safety master, then shot by shot.
2. Pause two seconds on each number before cutting, so the caption can sit on it.
3. Keep the cursor deliberate: every click is the one in the script.
4. Cut loading spinners and the first second of every transition.
5. Record the panel at 100 percent zoom; do not zoom the browser, crop in the edit instead.

## Beats that must be legible at a glance

- The colours replacing grey (shot 2).
- The route changing shape and number (shot 4).
- The page number on the citation (shot 5).

If one of those is not readable when the video is played at half size on a phone, re-record that shot rather than shrinking the font.

## What the video must not do

- Show a mockup, a slide or a placeholder frame as if it were the product.
- Claim predictive maintenance. The work order is derived from the service interval, and it says so.
- Present the synthetic plant as a real mill. Shot 1 and the closing line both name it.
