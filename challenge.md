## Shortlist (Thursday 5 Nov)

The summaries opened in sinceai.app at 15:00. This is a recommendation for the team to agree before Friday 15:00, when selection opens and places are first come and final.

Decision rule, in order: a working demo is possible in one night, the data is available and safe to handle, the story is clear in 20 seconds to a non-expert voter, and the prize and the company matter to us.

| # | Company | Challenge | Why we would accept | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | DNA | Responsible guidance for everyday digital safety | The easiest of the three to explain to the 1,000 voters: paste a suspicious message and get clear, safe next steps, with the guardrails visible in the demo. Privacy and responsible AI are part of the product, which is one of the five judging criteria. No special data needed to demo. | The work goes to DNA (not owned by the team). Safety advice must be careful and bounded. Ask DNA for real scam samples. |
| 2 | Fonecta | Keeping local business information accurate | Every voter has seen wrong opening hours. The demo can use real public data: take a listing, verify it against the business's own site, flag what is stale and suggest the fix. Obvious business value and a strong vote story. | The demo must not look like a scraper; use a small set, cite sources, and keep it responsible. |
| 3 | Turku Energia | Short-term energy forecasting under uncertainty | The most technical pick, best for a company evaluator: a forecast against a baseline, one honest number for the gain, and public data (Fingrid, Nord Pool) if the company's data is thin. | Needs a real baseline and metric, or it looks like a chart. Budget time for honest evaluation. |

If the team wants a sustainability story instead, Apetit or Lindström are the next two: both are easy to explain and hard to fake, so the demo has to be a real calculation.

FORCIT Group is the only challenge that needs a paper NDA signed on site at its Friday briefing (Drill and Blast Cycle Intelligence, €3,000 first prize). Its summary is deliberately content-free, so choosing it is choosing blind. Only put it on the shortlist if someone on the team owns industrial operations, geology or blasting experience.
